What is PAT testing?

Portable appliance testing is the inspection and testing of plug-in electrical equipment to check it is safe to use. It catches damaged cables, faulty plugs and worn equipment before they cause a shock or fire.

Is PAT testing a legal requirement?

No law names it directly, but employers, landlords and anyone in control of premises must keep electrical equipment safe. PAT testing is the recognised way to show that. See the law.

How often do appliances need testing?

It depends on the equipment and how it is used. A power tool on a building site needs checking far more often than a desk lamp in a quiet office. We recommend a sensible frequency for your site.
