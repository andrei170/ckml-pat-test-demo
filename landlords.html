Questions

What people ask

Is PAT testing a legal requirement for landlords?

No law names PAT testing directly, but landlords must keep the appliances they supply safe. Regular testing is the usual way to show that.

Is PAT testing the same as an EICR?

No. An EICR covers the fixed wiring. PAT testing covers the plug-in appliances you supply.

Can you deal with my tenants for access?

Yes, we can arrange access with tenants or your agent.
