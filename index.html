What you get

The paperwork your insurer asks for

Every appliance gets a visual inspection and, where appropriate, electrical safety tests with calibrated equipment. Results are logged digitally, backed up with printed reports and pass or fail labels, so the evidence is on hand if an insurer, inspector or tenant asks.

  • Itemised register: every appliance, location and result
  • Pass and fail labels on every item
  • Socket testing and microwave leakage checks where required, free
  • A sensible retest date for each item, not a blanket one
